Recognition here means a labelled instance has at least half its faces in a tool's union of claimed faces, regardless of predicted instance identity. No-face instances have no face claimed. Claimed faces measures target-face recall; false positives are claims outside the target classes as a share of all claims. Dimensions and feature typing are not evaluated by this union metric.

These numbers are not comparable with mfinstseg_baseline.json. The repo scorer matches instances one to one at IoU ≥ 0.5; its any_feature excludes only step_levels and risers. This comparison's Any feature excludes twelve families: flats, freeform_surfaces, plates, risers, step_levels, thin_wall_bodies, sheet_metal_bodies, polygonal_stock, circular_face_patterns, repeating_radial_profiles, cylindrical_profiles, turned_steps.

Scoring skips superseded_by records, retains source records, and selects class-1 holes with bottom == through; class 12 selects flat, drill_point or unknown bottoms. Pooled Holes takes their union. Stock uses quiddity's unclaimed faces (the complement of the selected union) as a proxy. Shafts uses only turned_steps; MFInstSeg has no shaft target class, so that row counts claims outside the target taxonomy, not geometric errors.
