# Stop volume queries when their existing answer is already settled

A volume pass currently keeps sampling after an unresolved line has already made its result unavailable. I stop that discarded work. For at-most queries, a measured material prefix may also settle rejection before the inset pass reaches air. Acceptance still finishes the required inset pass and retains the original empty/full conventions, exact-volume threshold and unanswered-line refusal.

The directed regressions compare the affected computations with their original
paths, including refusal and boundary cases. They do not replace full workspace
regression or strict snapshot reproduction on the standard ARM macOS runner.
No expected snapshots, reference bands or inventory floors are changed.
